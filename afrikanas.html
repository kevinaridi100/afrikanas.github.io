The atelier

Heritage, cut to the individual.

Afrikanas was founded on a simple premise: that the tailoring traditions of East Africa — hand-loomed cotton, considered proportion, cloth built for warmth and movement — belong in the same room as Savile Row and Naples.

Every commission begins with a conversation, not a size chart. Our cutters study how a client stands, sits, and travels before a single pattern is drawn. The result is a garment that disappears on the body, doing its work without asking to be noticed.

"We are not dressing an occasion. We are dressing a life that repeats itself, differently, every day."

Bespoke process

From first measure to final press.

i

Consultation

A private sitting at our Nairobi atelier or your residence, to discuss cloth, cut, and how the garment should live in your wardrobe.

ii

Pattern and first fitting

Your pattern is drafted by hand and cut in a basted trial garment, fitted and marked for adjustment.

iii

Construction

Full hand-finishing over six to ten weeks, with a second fitting for suits and outerwear.

iv

Delivery and after-care

Delivered by hand where possible, with complimentary adjustments for the life of the garment.
